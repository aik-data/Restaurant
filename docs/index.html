Paying for your catering

  1. 1

    No payment to request a quote

    Send your request and we'll call or email to confirm the date, headcount and menu.

  2. 2

    Pay by secure email invoice

    Once confirmed, we email you an invoice. Pay by credit or debit card from your phone or computer in about a minute.

  3. 3

    Deposits for big events

    Events of 50+ guests: a 25% deposit holds your date. The balance is invoiced 3 days before the event.

Prefer to pay in person? Stop by the restaurant during dinner hours, Tuesday – Saturday.

Notice

48 hours for most orders. One week for groups over 50.

Pickup or delivery

Free pickup at the restaurant. $25 delivery in the Tehachapi area. Ask us about Mojave, Cal City and Bakersfield.

Changes

Final headcount and menu due 3 days before your event.

Dietary needs

Vegetarian dishes on every package. Vegan and gluten-conscious options on request.
